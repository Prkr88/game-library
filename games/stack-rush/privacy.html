Stack Rush - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Stack Rush stores your scores, coins and settings locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit or share personal data, and the game makes no network calls while you play.

Advertising

If a version of the game shows ads, they are provided by a third-party ad network and only after you give consent where required (for example the EU consent form and iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt). You can decline, and the game stays fully playable. The ad provider may process your device advertising ID under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from children.
